## The defensible hackathon story

**Which customer need should change a product decision, what evidence supports it, who is missing, and what test could reverse that decision?**

SignalScope can demonstrate a short, inspectable chain:
1. Capture an original source and record how it was obtained.
2. Separate supplier claims, customer statements and analyst context.
3. Compare exact models on a shared set of user-selected criteria.
4. Quote the captured source for each supported cell; label gaps as unknown.
5. Propose one experiment, with existing company-pilot guardrails and outcome tracking.
6. Export a brief for the next PERLab workstream.

The code validates quote traceability. That does not validate interpretation, source truth, customer representativeness, economic impact or causal effect.

## PERLab alignment

[Kearney's PERLab overview](https://www.kearney.com/web/product-excellence-and-renewal-lab/) links Velocity AI, Tariff Simulator and Ingredient Compass. [PERL 2.0](https://perlab2xo.kearney.com/) describes design-for-growth, design-to-value, portfolio work and AEPD.

SignalScope covers the consumer-evidence part of deciding what to investigate. Its exported brief is a manual handoff, not an API integration, tariff forecast, ingredient assessment or reproduction of proprietary AEPD scoring.

Do not claim access to all Kearney apps or client data. Public product information does not expose their private schemas, credentials, methods or workspace APIs.

## Product competitor analysis workflow

Use the new `/research` page with two to four exact models and original product or customer sources. Enter the target market/version and criteria explicitly. Source discovery is a lead generator; review and capture the sources before comparing.

A cell labeled "Not found in captured evidence" means the selected source did not establish the answer. It never establishes that a competitor lacks a capability. Prices need a region, currency, date and comparable configuration; the system does not invent those fields or calculate market share.

## What to evaluate

- Supported-cell quote match rate (the app enforces exact substring matches).
- Human correctness judgments for those claims, not merely valid JSON.
- Unsupported inference rate versus a basic summarizer.
- Time to identify a defensible product experiment.
- Whether non-buyer and silent-user questions become explicit research tasks.

No benchmark results are claimed yet.
